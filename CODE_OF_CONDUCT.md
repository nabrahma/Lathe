# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the person you are replying to is trying to help.

## What is expected

Discussion here is about a file converter. Keep it there. Criticise code,
decisions and documentation as sharply as you like, and criticise people not at
all.

Concretely, that means no harassment, no personal attacks, no demeaning remarks
about anyone's identity or background, no unwelcome attention, and no posting
of anyone's private information.

It also means taking maintainer time seriously. Search before opening an issue,
say what you actually did and what actually happened, and accept that "not in
scope" is a legitimate answer.

## Scope

Issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages and any other space the
project uses, plus any place where you are representing the project.

## Reporting

Email nabaskarforcode99@gmail.com. Reports are read by the maintainer and kept
private.

Include what happened, where, and links if there are any. You will get a reply
within seven days.

## Enforcement

The maintainer decides, and the response fits what happened: a private word for
something careless, a public correction and edited content for something worse,
a temporary block for a repeated pattern, a permanent ban for harassment or for
a single serious incident.

If you think a decision about you was wrong, say so by email and it will be
looked at again.
